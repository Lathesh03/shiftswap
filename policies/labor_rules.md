# Labor & Scheduling Policy

## Overlapping shifts
An employee may not be scheduled for two shifts that overlap in time.

## Rest between shifts
An employee must have at least 8 hours of rest between the end of one
shift and the start of the next.

## Self-swaps
An employee cannot take over a shift they themselves requested to give away.

## Manager approval
Every shift swap must be approved by a manager before it is applied.

## Weekly hours
No employee may exceed 40 scheduled hours in a calendar week without
explicit manager sign-off.
